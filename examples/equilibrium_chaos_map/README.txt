This example computes the the Digit Accuracy of the WBA for all particles in the QA phase space.  
Weighted Birkhoff Averaging is added as a setting to the trapped map and applied to particle canonical momentum to create a numerical 
metric (Digit Accuracy) of chaos, implemented as described in:

N. Duignan and J. D. Meiss. "Distinguishing between regular and chaotic orbits of flows by the weighted birkhoff average." Physical Nonlinear Phenomena. (2023): 449:133749.
